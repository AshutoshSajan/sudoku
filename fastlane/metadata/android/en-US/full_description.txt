Sudoku is a beautiful, modern puzzle game that runs fully offline.
Every puzzle is generated on-device and verified to have exactly one solution.

Features:
• 4 difficulty levels: Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert
• Pencil-mark notes with automatic cleanup
• Hints on Easy and Medium (3 per game)
• Unlimited undo and one-tap erase
• Mistake tracking — 3 mistakes ends the game
• Timer with auto-pause
• Dark and light themes
• In-game How to Play guide

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Free and open source (GPL-3.0).
